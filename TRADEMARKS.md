# Trademark Policy

## The short version

The name **GlyphMark** and its logo belong to the project, not to whoever uses them. You may say
that your product "works with GlyphMark", "uses GlyphMark", or "supports marks produced by
GlyphMark". You may not name your product GlyphMark, or something that looks like it, and you may
not imply that the project endorses or certifies your product.

This mirrors the Linux Foundation / CNCF trademark policy for project marks, so that nothing has to
change if the project moves under a foundation.

## What the marks are

* **GlyphMark** — the project name, in that capitalisation, one word.
* **The GlyphMark logo** — `logo/glyphmark.svg` and `logo/glyphmark-mono.svg`.
* The word mark must be written `GlyphMark`. Do not hyphenate, split or restyle it as `Glyph Mark`,
  `GLYPHMARK`, or `glyph-mark`, except in a domain/package identifier where case is forced
  (`pip install glyphmark` is fine).

## Permitted uses

* Naming the tool accurately: `glyphmark detect`, `GlyphMark`, "the GlyphMark CLI".
* Documentation, articles, talks, courses and comparison pages that refer to the project honestly.
* Modified versions: you may say "based on GlyphMark", "a fork of GlyphMark", or "GlyphMark-derived"
  — and if you fork you must **change the name** in your distribution's user-facing UI, package
  metadata and docs.

## Not permitted without written permission

* Names for products, services, domains or accounts that incorporate the mark beyond truthful
  reference ("GlyphMark Cloud", "glyphmark.ai", `@glyphmark` on a social platform).
* Logos, iconography or colour schemes likely to be confused with ours.
* Stating or implying endorsement, sponsorship, certification or affiliation.
* Using the marks in advertising or on a storefront in a way that suggests the project recommends a
  commercial offering.

## Forks and redistribution

Redistributing unmodified source is fine, licence conditions aside (`LICENSE`, `NOTICE`). If you ship
a modified build: rename it, say what you changed, and do not present it as the project's build. The
project has no way to vouch for a modified build, and a modified build that mangles detection would
harm people who trust the name.

## Non-infringing uses

Descriptive, factual references, unmodified redistribution under the MIT licence, and use of the CLI
inside your own product or pipeline are non-infringing. Attribution is welcome and helps the
project: `Built with GlyphMark` with a link to the repository.

## Reporting misuse / asking for permission

Open an issue (private contact details in `SECURITY.md` if the matter is sensitive) or email a
maintainer from `MAINTAINERS.md`. We try to answer trademark questions in a week; most requests are
answered yes in writing.

## Enforcement

We start with a written request, in public where the misuse is public, and give a reasonable time to
comply. Legal escalation is a last resort, and never the first step with a community member who made
an honest mistake.
